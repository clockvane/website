# Enterprise-grade UI/UX audit: live site, 2026-11-15

Scope: https://clockvane-website.pages.dev/ (v1.0.0), all 10 sitemap pages plus the 404 page.
Method: `.claude/skills/design-quality/SKILL.md`. Type and layout scan with `design-scan.mjs` at 375,
768, 1280 and 1440 px; visual review of full-page screenshots at 375 and 1440 px; contrast computed
from `docs/design/tokens.json` and `docs/design/contrast-pairs.json`; axe (WCAG 2.0 A/AA, 2.1 and 2.2 AA tags)
on 11 URLs at 375 px; Lighthouse mobile and desktop on the 10 sitemap pages; keyboard focus check of
the mobile menu on the live site (`docs/engineering/evidence/2026-11-15-mobile-nav-focus.md`).
Screenshots and raw JSON stay in `.agentops-scratch/` and are not committed.

## 1. Summary

**Top 5 problems by visitor impact**

1. **The home "problem" paragraph is set as a display statement.** It is 357 characters at 28 px
   (36 px at 1440), weight 600, line height 1.1, letter spacing −0.025em. Visitors read the most
   important-looking text on the page as a headline, and it is a paragraph (F-01).
2. **Body text runs too wide.** Paragraphs measure 84–90 characters per line at 1440 px (target
   60–75) (F-02).
3. **Helper and note text is 14 px.** The "We use your details…" line, the mockup prompts and the
   closing notes are below the 16 px body role (F-03).
4. **Lead paragraphs read cramped.** Leads are 20 px at line height 1.3 on every page. When they wrap,
   the lines sit too close together (F-04).
5. **Every page logs a console error.** The favicon request returns 404, so Lighthouse best practices
   scores 96 on every page (F-10).

**Target state (two sentences).** Each text role has one size, measure and leading, drawn from the
tokens: display only for one-sentence headlines, body and lead for everything else, at 60–75
characters per line. The site keeps its current accessibility and performance (axe 0; Lighthouse
performance and accessibility 100; best practices 100 after #225), and adds the trust pages and the
mobile next step that careful buyers look for.
