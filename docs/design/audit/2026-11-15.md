# Enterprise-grade UI/UX audit: live site, 2026-11-15

Scope: https://clockvane-website.pages.dev/ (v1.0.0), all 10 sitemap pages plus the 404 page.
Method: `.claude/skills/design-quality/SKILL.md`. Type and layout scan with `design-scan.mjs` at 375,
768, 1280 and 1440 px; visual review of full-page screenshots at 375 and 1440 px; contrast computed
from `docs/design/tokens.json` and `docs/design/contrast-pairs.json`; axe (WCAG 2.0 A/AA, 2.1 and 2.2 AA tags)
on 11 URLs at 375 px; Lighthouse mobile and desktop on the 10 sitemap pages.
Screenshots and raw JSON stay in `.agentops-scratch/` and are not committed.

## 1. Summary

**Top 5 problems by visitor impact**

1. **The home "problem" paragraph is set as a display statement.** It is 357 characters at 28 px
   (36 px at 1440), weight 600, line height 1.1, letter spacing −0.025em. Visitors read the most
   important-looking text on the page as a headline, and it is a paragraph (F-01).
2. **Body text runs too wide.** Paragraphs measure 84–90 characters per line at 1440 px (target
   60–75) (F-02).
3. **Helper and note text is 14 px.** The "We use your details…" line, the mockup prompts and the
   closing notes are below the 16 px body role (F-03).
4. **Mobile navigation links can take focus while nearly invisible.** At 375 px, seven links are
   4–16 px wide. This fails the 24 px target size and may trap keyboard users on controls they
   cannot see (F-16). Lead paragraphs at 20 px and line height 1.3 are a lesser issue (F-04).
5. **The contact details look personal, not corporate.** The published support address is a Gmail
   address. Enterprise buyers check this first (F-15).

**Target state (two sentences).** Each text role has one size, measure and leading, drawn from the
tokens: display only for one-sentence headlines, body and lead for everything else, at 60–75
characters per line. The site keeps its current accessibility and performance (axe 0, Lighthouse
100), and adds the trust pages and the mobile next step a buyer expects.

## 4. Plan

**Phase 1: Foundations**
- #222: role-based type scale, body measure and leading, site-wide (`tokens.json`,
  `design-system.md`, `components.md`). Fixes F-01 (style), F-04, F-06, F-08.

**Phase 2: Components and copy**
- #223: home problem copy and body paragraph (F-01, F-05). Depends on #222. Owner reviews the copy.
- #224: measure token, body and helper sizes, lead leading, caption role (F-02, F-03, F-04, F-07).
  Depends on #222.
- #225: favicon and console error (F-10). No dependency.
- #226: section rhythm from spacing tokens (F-11). Depends on #222.
- #229: mobile navigation focus and target sizes (F-16). Depends on #222.

**Phase 3: Pages**
- Covered by #224 across every page. Re-run the scan after Phase 2 and record results here in a
  follow-up audit.

**Phase 4: Features (P3)**
- #227: accessibility statement page (F-12). Depends on #222.
- #228: sticky mobile action bar (F-13). Depends on #222.

**Owner decisions (not tickets)**
- OQ-1: contact email and phone (F-15). Recommendation: a company address on the company domain
  once the domain is live. Until then, keep the current address. Nothing is invented (rule 9).
- OQ-2: the Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon (F-14). Recommendation: keep it only if the owner
  confirms the cookieless analytics setup, because it is the one existing third-party request.
