# Current-site research

Reviewed 2026-10-29, before concept selection. Twelve live homepages: three AI, three developer-tool, three cloud, three design agencies. Read their public pages and captured a 1440 × 1000 desktop viewport in Chromium with reduced motion. Observations are a design reading of those captures, not performance or accessibility audits. Some experiences were still loading after the initial capture; those limits are called out. No assets, layouts, slogans or code are copied into the prototypes.

| Site | What works in the inspected page | Principle to borrow | What to avoid for this brief |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/) · AI | Large editorial statement, disciplined margins and a clear shift into a report feature. Serif and sans distinguish explanation from navigation. | Give the business idea space before asking someone to decode a diagram. In B, use a human voice against precise system notation. | The long institutional introduction and collage would bury a small-business conversion. No research-company positioning. |
| [Mistral](https://mistral.ai/) · AI | A recognisable warm pixel vocabulary and fine grid make separate areas feel related. One contact action is easy to find. | Give all six services the same visual grammar. A single original shape can identify the system. | The capture caught fragmented headline letters during an entrance sequence; our headline must never wait for motion. Avoid its pixel mascot and partner-logo proof. |
| [Cohere](https://cohere.com/) · AI | A short autonomy-focused message leads directly into a product interface. The illustration is grounded in something a buyer could use. | Explain who controls the process; show the person-check path in AI examples. | Large product screenshots can become illegible on phones. Do not imply we sell a finished enterprise AI platform. |
| [Linear](https://linear.app/) · developer tool | Very economical navigation and a strong dark canvas. The page text describes a coherent planning/building system. | Limit hierarchy levels and keep technical detail connected to a task. | Our reduced-motion first capture showed the header above an empty hero. We cannot establish the cause, but will explicitly test immediate content and static fallbacks. Do not imitate its product UI or claim an accessibility defect from this capture alone. |
| [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) · developer platform | An extremely spare horizontal composition: proposition, central identity, then short use cases. CTA contrast is unambiguous. | Use alignment and clear choices instead of ornament to signal engineering quality (A). | Brand scale and logo walls carry much of its credibility; we have no comparable evidence and will not fabricate it. |
| [Resend](https://resend.com/) · developer tool | Strong typography and a single lit object make a spacious dark hero memorable. The product benefit stays close to the CTA. | Reserve the strongest light/depth moment for one explanatory hero (C). | The rendered cube is not an explanation of our work. Avoid generic 3D objects, background video and borrowing its object or headline composition. |
| [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/) · cloud | One vivid, contained hero region with a direct start action; broad infrastructure is framed as a customer benefit. | Keep the service promise and action readable above any visual activity. | Massive scale statistics and animated media are not transferable. No implied platform-scale track record. |
| [Fly.io](https://fly.io/) · cloud | Distinctive illustrated worlds give infrastructure a friendly tone; typography breaks up a technical category. | Original diagrams can make engineering approachable without looking generic. | Spacecraft and planets would turn this brief into a sci-fi cliché. Our direction cue must be a business route, not a space journey. |
| [Railway](https://railway.com/) · cloud | Calm promise above a visible infrastructure canvas; the product model and message reinforce each other. | Draw the connections between tools instead of listing disconnected capabilities. | Scenic full-bleed art and purple atmosphere would add page weight and drift toward the banned generic AI look. |
| [Work & Co](https://work.co/) · agency | Strong left/right alignment, short positioning and restrained dividers make the opening easy to scan. | Use fewer elements and make each section answer one buyer question (A). | The latest-work media was blank at capture time. We cannot rely on portfolio media or client claims to fill the page. |
| [Instrument](https://www.instrument.com/) · agency | Confident oversized type and a coherent media composition make the opening feel authored. | Let scale create drama; C can be bold without a rendering engine. | A huge wordmark and reel would postpone the service explanation. No autoplay or borrowed fashion imagery. |
| [BASIC/DEPT](https://www.basicagency.com/) · agency | A carefully art-directed opening with simple overlaid navigation and a conspicuous reel control. | One focal moment is stronger than six competing effects. | Film-led proof, cursor gimmicks and oversized reel navigation are poor fits for the budget and audit-call path. Use an explicit, accessible replay button instead. |

## Synthesis into the three concepts

- **A — Quiet control:** Vercel's economy and Work & Co's alignment become a readable order-routing board, with none of their brand or portfolio dependencies.
- **B — The routing dial:** Anthropic's human typography and Cohere/Railway's explanatory product thinking become a labelled vane choosing the next business step. No opaque AI spectacle.
- **C — Dispatch field:** Resend's focused lighting and Instrument's confident scale become a perspective diagram of a real workflow. The light trail moves a task toward its destination; it is not an ambient loop.

All three borrow principles, use the existing business copy, put the call to action before the visual, and keep meaning available as plain HTML. The service examples are original SVGs. The comparison is a design hypothesis for the owner to choose at Gate 2b, not user-research evidence.
