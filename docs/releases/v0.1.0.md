## Rollout and rollback
Production deploys go through the `production` environment, and the owner approves. To roll back, redeploy the previous
tag with `rollback.yml`, or roll back the deployment in the Cloudflare dashboard (cicd.md § Rollback). For this first
release there is no earlier production deployment. Rolling back means removing the custom domain or leaving `pages.dev`
on the last good deployment. Steps: [release.md § First go-live](../devops/release.md#first-go-live-v100).
