## What went well
- Staging deploy stabilised: retro 3 had 4 failures in 10 runs, now none in 11. Failures were configuration, and fixes landed (#206 to #209).
- Release went through the designed path: checklist (#190, #217/#219), staging enquiry evidence (#211), manual keyboard pass (#218), Gate 3 (#212), then `release` run success.
- The one-push-per-round rule (#188) held: panels stayed small and no PR needed escalation.
- The audit (#221) turned into small, token-based PRs (#222, #224, #226, #227, #229, #231) rather than one large change.

## What slowed us down
- **Production enquiry path.** Three fix PRs after the Worker split (#206, #208, #209) before staging evidence (#211) was clean; the missing production origin was caught only by a new release guard (#207).
- **Content after approval.** Owner answers changed copy and contact details late (#192, #198, #213, #241), each a separate small PR.
- **Owner handoffs.** Workflow, release and gate PRs wait for the owner, as designed; Gate 3 spanned about two days from the checklist (#190) to merge.
