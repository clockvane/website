# Instructions for AI agents in this repository

This website is built by the Clockvane agent team. **`CLAUDE.md` is the team constitution and applies
to every agent, whatever model or tool runs it** (Claude Code, Codex or anything else). Read it in full
before you do anything and follow it exactly; where it says "Claude", read "you".

- Your role and your work order are in the prompt you were started with. Do only that work order.
- The procedures the constitution calls skills are in `.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`
  (`delivery-protocol` for implement/fix, `review-protocol` for review).
- Governance files are human-only: `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `.claude/`, `.codex/`, `agentops/`,
  `.github/CODEOWNERS`, `docs/product/BRIEF.md`.
